# HaloNotch — build-in-public launch kit

Free Mac app that turns the notch into a Dynamic Island. The hook is Now Playing:
your album art spins like a vinyl record, and you can **tap it to swap to a
cassette or a CD**. Plus the camera's literally behind the notch, so there's a
mirror.

## The video (9:16, ~15–25s, screen recording)

Record with **Cmd+Shift+5 → "Record Selected Portion"** over a clean wallpaper.
Crop to vertical after. No talking — let the motion carry it.

Shot list:
1. **(0–3s)** Music playing. Hover the notch → tray drops, vinyl spinning. Hold a beat.
2. **(3–7s)** Tap the record → flips to a **cassette** (reels spinning). Tap again → **CD** (rainbow shimmer).
3. **(7–11s)** Hit **pause** → record eases to a stop, tonearm lifts. Play → spins back up.
4. **(11–17s)** Tap the **camera button** → tray becomes a live mirror. Wave. Tap to close.
5. **(last 2s)** On-screen text: "free + open source — link in bio."
